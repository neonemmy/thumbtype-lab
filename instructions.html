THUMBTYPE LAB

How the test works

This experiment compares touch placement, error rate and typing speed using your left thumb, right thumb, and both thumbs.

Before you begin

  • Hold the phone naturally, as you normally would while typing.
  • Follow the blue thumb indicator for each test.
  • Type the displayed phrase on the on-screen test keyboard.
  • Each thumb mode is repeated three times.
  • Do not deliberately slow down to improve accuracy — type at a comfortable natural pace.

Mistakes are useful data

Do not stop or restart because of a wrong key. The test records substitutions, skipped characters, extra taps, touch position, and typing speed.

Sound feedback

Button and typing feedback sounds follow the iPhone Silent Mode setting. If you want audio feedback, turn Silent Mode off. The test still works normally with sound disabled.

Your device

Web apps cannot reliably ask iOS for the exact iPhone model. ThumbType Lab can make a best-effort guess from screen and viewport metrics. When multiple models share a class, it prefers the newest plausible model. The model remains editable in the test.

The raw screen/viewport measurements are also saved with test results, which is more useful scientifically than relying only on a guessed model name.

THUMBTYPE LAB

How the test works

This experiment compares touch placement, error rate and typing speed using your left thumb, right thumb, and both thumbs.

Before you begin

  • Hold the phone naturally, as you normally would while typing.
  • Follow the blue thumb indicator for each test.
  • Type the displayed phrase on the on-screen test keyboard.
  • Each thumb mode is repeated three times.
  • Do not deliberately slow down to improve accuracy — type at a comfortable natural pace.

Mistakes are useful data

Do not stop or restart because of a wrong key. The test records substitutions, skipped characters, extra taps, touch position, and typing speed.

Sound feedback

A tap on the highlighted key plays a short, bright rising chirp. A different key or a gap plays a brief, sad descending cue. Any position inside the intended key counts as good; touch placement is still measured separately. Each new tap replaces the previous sound, and there is no sustained tone between taps. Feedback uses the highlighted key before the tap; later error alignment can assign that tap differently.

During typing, an original 8-bit game loop plays when Music is on. The nine repetitions are levels: the tune gets faster and adds notes as you advance. Use the Music button to turn it off or on; the choice is saved on this device. The music pauses between repetitions and stops when the app is hidden. Finishing each repetition plays a rising level-up tune. Completing all nine repetitions plays a longer victory fanfare. These follow the last tap, and pressing Go, restarting, or leaving the app stops them.

Before testing, check that the tones are comfortable at your chosen volume and Silent Mode setting, both in Safari and from the Home Screen.

Your device

Web apps cannot reliably ask iOS for the exact iPhone model or OS version. ThumbType Lab ranks plausible models from screen and viewport metrics. Safari may report a frozen iOS version, so correct either field if you know the actual value.

Your selections are saved on this device and used by the test results. The raw screen/viewport measurements are still saved separately.
